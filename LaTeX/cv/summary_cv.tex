\cvsection{Profile}

\begin{cvparagraph}
    Radar systems engineer working across the full stack of automotive sensing---waveform and MIMO array architecture, mm-wave front ends, and the detection and estimation chain that turns them into perception. Ph.D.\ work built five generations of portable multi-mode radar hardware at C-, K- and 24-GHz bands, including the first PCB realization of a 2D K-band beamforming localization radar; industrial work since 2018 has taken 4D imaging radar and interior sensing radar from concept into production at Aptiv.

    \vspace{1.2mm}
    The record spans 1 edited book, 3 book chapters, 16 journal articles, 32 conference papers and 10 granted patents, cited 2,300+ times (h-index 24), alongside sustained peer-review service as an Associate Editor for \emph{IEEE Transactions on Radar Systems} and, for five years, \emph{IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement}. A parallel thread is open-source infrastructure for the field: \cvurl{https://radarsimx.com/}{RadarSimX}, a GPU-accelerated radar simulation toolchain, and the analysis and visualization tools around it.
\end{cvparagraph}
