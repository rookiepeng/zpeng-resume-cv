\cvsection{Profile}

\begin{cvparagraph}
    Radar systems engineer working across the full stack of automotive sensing---waveform and MIMO array architecture, mm-wave front ends, and the detection and estimation chain that turns them into perception. Doctoral work built five generations of portable multi-mode radar hardware; industrial work since 2018 has taken 4D imaging radar and interior sensing radar from concept into production at Aptiv.

    \vspace{1.4mm}
    The record spans \cvBooks{} edited book, \cvChapters{} book chapters, \cvJournals{} journal articles, \cvConfPapers{} conference papers and \cvPatents{} granted patents, cited \cvCitations{} times (h-index \cvHIndex), alongside sustained peer-review service as an Associate Editor for \emph{IEEE Transactions on Radar Systems} and, for five years, \emph{IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement}. A parallel thread is open-source infrastructure for the field: \cvurl{https://radarsimx.com/}{RadarSimX}, a GPU-accelerated radar simulation toolchain, and the analysis and visualization tools around it.
\end{cvparagraph}
